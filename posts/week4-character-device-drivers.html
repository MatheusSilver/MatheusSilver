Introduction to Linux kernel Character Device Drivers

Objective

This week we did not follow a full tutorial. Instead, we focused on understanding character device drivers directly.

For that purpose, we divided the topic into a few steps:

  • Theoretical explanation of major and minor numbers (including a short in-class lecture)
  • Installation of a simple_char driver
  • Analysis of the results

Challenges

I did not encounter significant issues this week, so there is no major blocker to report.

Experience

We learned how Linux exposes many interactions through files and how to design safe read/write operations. We also discussed how to use structs to organize driver logic and define operation behavior clearly.
