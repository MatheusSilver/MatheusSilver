Finally contributing to Plasma Desktop

Introduction

Last week we finally got plasma-desktop building locally, and that enabling us to proceed to the second step of the roadmap we picked, improving how gaming mice show up in the Mouse KCM. This week, we finally managed to send our contribution to Plasma, after spending almost two weeks trying to figure out how to even compile Plasma, !3896 - Group mice by group ID in the device list.

The problem

Some physical mice, especially the ones with extra buttons and receivers, are read by the system as more than one logical input device. Since the Mouse KCM listed every logical device it found, the same physical mouse could show up twice, or even more, in the settings list, which is confusing when you just want to configure "your mouse".

The fix

libinput already groups logical devices that belong to the same physical hardware under a device group id, but KWin just wasn't exposing it yet. The merge request modifies four files to expose that id and use it to deduplicate the list. Here is each change explained.

Put together, only one entry per physical mouse shows up now, no matter how many logical devices libinput reports for it, and a mouse plugged in mid-session is deduplicated the same way as one that was already connected at login.

Conclusion

This closes the second step of the gaming mice roadmap, devices are grouped correctly now, which is the base that button remapping and per-device settings need to make sense. The merge request is open and waiting on review, and next up is looking into button remapping itself, however, this third step is probably going to be executed by EACH students who actually wrote the roadmap itself.
