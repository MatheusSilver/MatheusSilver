New Projects

Short summary

This week we were presented with 11 different projects to potentially contribute to in the second phase of the course — one of them being the Linux Kernel itself, which we had already worked on during Phase 1.

The projects were:

  • Linux Kernel: In summary, continue from phase 1
  • Debian
  • KDE
  • GNOME
  • Git
  • QEMU
  • kw (kworkflow)
  • Patch-hub
  • ArKanjo
  • Cregit
  • ML-Heritage

Our Choice: KDE

After reviewing all the options, we decided to contribute to KDE. The choice was driven by two factors. First, we already had prior experience using the KDE Plasma desktop environment, which gave us some familiarity with the project and its community. Second, and perhaps more importantly, we were drawn to the opportunity to work on software that is being used by the gaming community, specifically the Steam Deck, and more recently, Steam Machine as well.
